# Strategy research sources used by the local agent lab

These sources seed hypotheses only. The lab does not assume a published idea
will work on Codan or ASX data; each idea must pass the local fee-inclusive,
chronological tests.

- Jason McIntosh / Motion Trader: breakout consolidation, ATR trailing stops,
  and medium-term trend following:
  https://www.motiontrader.com.au/resources/investment-insights/3-trailing-stop-strategies-and-how-to-use-them-yourself/
- Motion Trader investment process:
  https://www.motiontrader.com.au/our-investment-process
- Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen, *Time Series Momentum* (Journal of Financial
  Economics, 2012):
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X11002613
- Donchian channel research and trend-regime framing:
  https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/9781119207801.ch15

The lab also supports novel combinations of causal indicators. “Novel” means
newly generated for the experiment; it does not mean the result is validated or
that the idea has never appeared elsewhere.
